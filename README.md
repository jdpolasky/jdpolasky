# Joshua Polasky

**Business first, then AI.**

I'm a strategy and operations consultant and the founder of Prajna Group. Most businesses know they need AI but have no idea where to start. If you lead with AI and the business has bad processes or bottlenecks, AI just makes those problems happen faster. So I start with the business. I look at the whole operation, find the bottlenecks and the broken processes, and fix those first. Then I show where AI fits, what to do first, and who should build it.

My whole career has been fixing businesses. I led a 40-employee manufacturing company through federal bankruptcy and out the other side as a profitable business. I rebuilt sales and marketing at a regional services franchise and grew sales 11%, 7%, and 8% three years running. I steadied a newly acquired company through its first year under new ownership, from supply chain to a full software changeover to coaching its frontline leaders. I've worked across manufacturing, cleantech, professional services, and tech.

## Work with me

If you own or lead a business, know AI matters, and don't know where to begin, or you need a fractional operator, get in touch. It starts with a 20-minute call, no pitch. ## Why there's code here

I build my own AI tools with Claude Code, with no coding background, so I know what they can and can't do in a real business. They started with my own ADHD. Every productivity system I tried fell apart on me, so I built one that works the way my brain does. Everything below is my own work, open source.

### [chief-of-staff-2](https://github.com/jdpolasky/chief-of-staff-2) ![stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/jdpolasky/chief-of-staff-2?style=flat)

An AI assistant that keeps track of your calendar, your open commitments, and your standing rules for how you work, and gives you a morning brief. Plain markdown in an Obsidian vault, ADHD-first, open to anyone. This is the current version, so start here.

### Also public

- [discord-digest-bot](https://github.com/jdpolasky/discord-digest-bot). A small bot that reads what a Discord server talked about and posts a short summary back, daily and weekly. It ships with 100 automated tests, and its README spells out the safety checks.
- [bloatbot](https://github.com/jdpolasky/bloatbot). Audits your AI workspace for bloat and proposes cuts. It changes nothing without your say-so.
- [buildbot](https://github.com/jdpolasky/buildbot). A builder agent. It interviews you, puts a one-page blueprint in front of you, builds only what you approve, and runs the result end to end while you watch.
- [ai-chief-of-staff-engine](https://github.com/jdpolasky/ai-chief-of-staff-engine). A tested memory engine, built in eight stages, with automated tests on Windows and Linux. My own daily system runs on plain markdown instead.
